Each block is one run of the device, from power-on to power-off. Click one to zoom to it.
Times and dates

The WireView has no clock, so it only records time since power-on. Dates come from your PC's Windows power history when it's loaded. You can also enter when a power-on ended, or use Ended just before next if the next one began right after (e.g. a firmware update). A time you enter always wins.
